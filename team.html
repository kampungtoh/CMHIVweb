團隊 Team 05

友善醫療不是一個人的事。 Friendly care is never one person's job.

這是一支橫跨臨床、護理、行政、品管、感管、牙科、外科、婦產、小兒、ICU 與長照窗口的團隊。 This is a team that spans clinical care, nursing, administration, quality, infection control, dentistry, surgery, OB-GYN, paediatrics, ICU, and long-term care liaisons.

把友善照護放進品質與病安治理結構中持續監看與改善。Embedding friendly care into the quality and patient safety governance structure.

在每一次接觸中決定「友善」是真實的還是只是文件上的字。In every encounter, decides whether "friendly" is real or only on paper.
控

安全與尊嚴並重Safety with Dignity
用實證設計感控流程，避免污名化與資源浪費同時發生。Evidence-based infection control designed to avoid both stigma and waste.

跨部門合作夥伴 Cross-departmental Partners

牙
牙科Dentistry
以實證標準提供友善牙科服務，破解最常見的就醫障礙之一。Evidence-based friendly dental services tackling one of the most common access barriers.
外
外科Surgery
擇期與急診手術皆設計友善流程與保護措施。Friendly workflows for both elective and emergency surgical care.

兒
小兒Paediatrics
處理母嬰垂直傳染預防與兒童感染照護的延續性服務。Mother-to-child transmission prevention and continuity care for paediatric infections.
重
重症 ICUIntensive Care
提供入住 ICU 的 PLHIV 同等級照護與隱私保護。Equal-quality ICU care with strong privacy protection for people living with HIV.
行
行政與公關Administration & PR
負責對內溝通、對外發聲，確保訊息一致而尊重。Internal communication and external voice — ensuring messaging stays consistent and respectful.
長
長照與社區Long-Term & Community Care
作為與長照機構與社區夥伴間的轉介與培訓窗口。Bridge for referral and training between long-term care facilities and community partners.
教
教育與訓練Education & Training
負責新進人員教育與年度回流訓練，把友善寫進入職第一週。Onboarding and annual refresh training — writing friendliness into staff's first week.
資

究
