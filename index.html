奇美醫院 · HIV 友善醫療推動計畫 · 自 2018 起 A Chi Mei Hospital Initiative · Since 2018

友善醫療不是例外，
是我們對健康平權的承諾。 Friendly care is not an exception
— it is our commitment to health equity.

在奇美醫院，HIV 友善醫療始於「以人為本」——讓每一位走進來的人被看見、被陪伴、被尊重；同時也是治理責任、健康平權承諾，與資源意識型照護設計的挑戰。從友善醫療出發，走向系統重設、公共衛生與長期照護。 At Chi Mei Hospital, HIV-friendly care begins with the person — each one seen, accompanied and respected. It is also a governance responsibility, a health-equity commitment, and a resource-conscious care-design challenge — moving from friendly care to system redesign, public health, and long-term care readiness.
