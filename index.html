Film Festival Retrospective

2025 Featured Film

Eyes on Ukraine

Dir. Mo Stoebe
37 min
Documentary

This film follows 24-year-old Ukrainian HIV activist Yana Panfilova, who continues to lead the youth organization Teenergizer in Berlin after being displaced by war. She and her partners return to Ukraine after training, using images to reveal the unique public health crisis during wartime—a moving record of human rights, disease, and resilience.

Executive Producer: Richard Gere | Global Premiere Season (Taiwan Stop)
