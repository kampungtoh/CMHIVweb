關於計畫 About 01

從友善到平權，從活動到系統。 From friendliness to equity, from events to systems.

我們不把「友善」視為一場感性活動，而是一套可被治理、可被測量、可被持續改善的照護系統。 We do not treat friendliness as a sentimental campaign. We treat it as a care system that can be governed, measured, and continuously improved.

「在奇美醫院，HIV 友善醫療是一項治理責任、健康平權承諾，也是資源意識型照護設計的挑戰。」 "At Chi Mei Hospital, HIV-friendly healthcare is a governance responsibility, a health equity commitment, and a resource-conscious care design challenge."

為何重要 Why it matters

看見污名背後的系統問題 Stigma is a system problem

病人經驗到的不友善，往往不是某位人員的態度問題，而是流程、空間、資訊與制度設計沒有把他們考慮進去。 Unfriendly experiences are rarely about a single staff member's attitude — they reflect workflows, spaces, information, and policies that were never designed with these patients in mind.

ESG 定位 ESG positioning

健康平權是社會責任 Health equity as social responsibility

作為區域教學醫院，我們把 HIV 友善照護放在 ESG 中的「S」核心，視為對社區、員工、與弱勢族群長期承擔的責任。 As a regional teaching hospital, HIV-friendly care sits at the heart of the "S" in our ESG framework — a long-term responsibility to the community, staff, and underserved groups.

五重目標 Quintuple Aim

把平權寫進品質定義 Equity, written into quality

我們依循 Quintuple Aim：病人體驗、人口健康、成本、員工幸福，加上「健康平權」。HIV 友善醫療同時對五個面向產生影響。 We follow the Quintuple Aim — patient experience, population health, cost, workforce well-being, and health equity. HIV-friendly care advances all five at once.

病人旅程 Patient Journey 02

點選任一階段，看看那一天我們做了什麼、病人感受到什麼、以及背後的設計選擇。 Click any stage to see what happens that day, what the patient experiences, and the design choices behind it.

第 0 天 · 09:00Day 0 · 09:00

匿名快篩，20 分鐘出結果Anonymous rapid test, results in 20 minutes

不需要證件、不需要解釋自己為什麼來。報到只需取一個編號，等待空間獨立規劃，全程不會被詢問私人關係或行為細節。No ID required. No need to explain why you came. Patients receive a number at check-in, wait in a privacy-protected space, and are never asked about partners or behaviour as a condition of testing.

背後的設計選擇Behind-the-scenes design
  • 櫃台與護理人員接受去污名語言訓練Reception and nursing trained in non-stigmatising language
  • 獨立報到動線，不混在一般候診區Separate check-in route, not mixed with general waiting area
  • 採檢空間採用單向流動，避免眼神接觸One-way flow in the testing room minimises unwanted eye contact
第 0 天 · 10:30Day 0 · 10:30

確診當下不獨自承受No one is alone at the moment of diagnosis

陽性結果出現時，諮詢師立刻在小房間內陪伴，不需轉介、不需排隊、不需另外掛號。整個確認與會談在 90 分鐘內完成。When the result is positive, a counsellor is immediately present in a private room — no referral wait, no queue, no separate registration. Confirmation and counselling complete within 90 minutes.

背後的設計選擇Behind-the-scenes design
  • 當日預留個案管理師時段Case manager has pre-reserved same-day slots
  • 會談室與一般診間分流，避免身分曝露Counselling room separated from general clinic flow to protect identity
  • 檢驗系統與感染科即時連線Lab and ID clinic systems connected in real time
第 0 天 · 14:00Day 0 · 14:00

當天就開始治療，不再多等一週Treatment begins the same day, not the following week

第一劑抗病毒藥物在診間服下，藥師同步衛教副作用與處置方式。病人帶著一張個案管理師的直撥電話卡片離開。The first dose of ART is taken in the clinic. The pharmacist explains side effects and how to handle them. The patient leaves with a card containing the case manager's direct line.

背後的設計選擇Behind-the-scenes design
  • 藥局保留同日 ART 配藥能力Pharmacy reserves same-day ART dispensing capacity
  • 藥事衛教整合到診間，免除二次跑點Medication counselling integrated into the clinic, no second trip required
  • 處方系統內建友善劑型決策樹Prescription system has built-in friendly regimen decision tree
第 7 天Day 7

電話、訊息、面談——病人選方式Phone, message, in-person — the patient chooses

第一週追蹤不強迫回診。病人可以選擇電話、加密訊息、或回診面談。漏接也不會被標記為「不配合」。The week-one follow-up is never mandatory in person. Patients choose phone, encrypted message, or visit. A missed contact is never flagged as "non-adherent".

背後的設計選擇Behind-the-scenes design
  • 病人主導的追蹤路徑（patient-led）Patient-led follow-up channel selection
  • 系統提示語言去懲罰化System prompts use non-punitive language
  • 追蹤記錄與一般病歷分流Follow-up records routed separately from general charts
3-6 個月Months 3-6

病毒量不可測，但人不被縮減Undetectable viral load — without reducing the person

當病毒量降到不可測，我們不會用「成功個案」這種把病人變成樣本的語言。回饋是：「你做到了你想做的，現在我們繼續一起照顧你的整體健康。」When viral load reaches undetectable, we never use "success case" language that turns the patient into a specimen. The message is: "You did what you wanted to do — now let's keep caring for your whole health together."

背後的設計選擇Behind-the-scenes design
  • 儀表板呈現去個人化、聚合資料Dashboards show de-identified, aggregated data
  • 慢性病、心理、社政整合進長期照護計畫Chronic care, mental health, and social services integrated into long-term plan
  • U=U 衛教納入病人自主時序U=U counselling delivered on the patient's own timeline
6 個月後 · 持續Month 6+ · Ongoing

U=U 不只是感染科的事U=U is not only the ID clinic's concern

病人回到牙科、外科、ICU、長照——每一個接觸點都應該知道 U=U 是什麼意思。我們建立的是一套讓病人不必反覆解釋自己的系統。When patients move on to dentistry, surgery, ICU, or long-term care, every touchpoint should know what U=U means. What we build is a system where patients never have to re-explain themselves.

背後的設計選擇Behind-the-scenes design

推動歷程 Journey 03

這不是一場活動，是十年的累積。 Not a campaign — a decade in the making.

從看見污名、走向倡議、進入測量，再走到 same-day、one-stop 與長期照護延伸，每一步都是有意識的設計選擇。 From recognising stigma to advocacy, measurement, same-day, one-stop, and long-term care extension — each step has been a deliberate design choice.

活動歷程 Programme Milestones 06

從問卷，到全院 campaign，到友善醫療日。 From survey, to hospital-wide campaign, to Friendly Medicine Day.

2018–19 年的問卷調查，讓我們第一次量出友善的距離；2020 年的全院 campaign 把它變成全院的事；2024 起以友善醫療日的形式，每年公開聽見、回應、改變。 In 2018–19 a hospital survey first measured our distance from friendliness. In 2020 a hospital-wide campaign made it everyone's job. From 2024, Friendly Medicine Day has carried it forward as an annual public moment to listen, respond, and change.

2024 · 第一屆友善醫療日 2024 · The first Friendly Medicine Day

第一屆友善醫療日將多年累積的友善照護與教育，正式以「醫療日」的形式對外呈現，邀請民眾、社群夥伴與院內同仁，一起把友善醫療帶到嘉年華現場。 The first Friendly Medicine Day brought years of friendly-care and education work into a public-facing format, inviting community partners, the public and our staff to share in a carnival of friendly healthcare.

2021–2023 · COVID-19 疫情期間的 HIV 照護 2021–2023 · HIV care during the COVID-19 pandemic

疫情打亂了所有人，但友善照護不能斷。我們在門禁、PPE 與隔離規則之中，重新設計就醫流程，讓 HIV 病人在最辛苦的三年裡仍然能拿到藥、被看見、被陪伴。 COVID-19 disrupted everyone — but friendly care could not stop. Working through visitor restrictions, PPE protocols and isolation rules, we redesigned care pathways so people living with HIV could still get their medication, be seen and be supported through the hardest three years.

挑戰Challenge疫情下的連續照護Continuity under COVID-19
重點Focus不中斷 ART・心理支持Uninterrupted ART · mental support
期間Period3 年years
疫情下的調整Pandemic adaptations

照護流程不中斷Keeping care unbroken

  • 遠距諮詢與電話追蹤，減少進院風險Tele-consultations and phone follow-up to reduce hospital exposure
  • 慢箋延長處方與郵寄/代領藥安排Extended prescriptions, mail or proxy pickup of ART
  • 針對隔離期間的個案管理特殊流程Dedicated case-management workflow during isolation
看見的需要Needs we saw

疫情放大了原有的不友善Pandemic amplified pre-existing gaps

  • 病人擔心 HIV 身份在篩檢/隔離流程曝光Patients feared HIV status being exposed in screening or isolation
  • 心理壓力上升，孤立感加劇Heightened psychological strain and isolation
  • 同住家屬的陪伴與支持也受到限制Family-based support was constrained by visitor rules
韌性與成果Resilience & outcomes

三年內維持治療連續性Sustained continuity of care over 3 years

  • ART 用藥不中斷，病毒抑制率穩定No interruption in ART; viral suppression remained stable
  • 建立疫情友善 SOP，後續沿用至 2024–25Pandemic-friendly SOPs that carried into 2024–25
  • 把心理支持與遠距照護常態化Normalised mental-health support and remote care

2020 · 全院友善醫療 Campaign 2020 · Hospital-wide Friendly Medicine Campaign

把問卷的發現轉化成行動。我們發起全院規模的友善醫療 campaign，讓「友善」不再只屬於感染科，而是奇美所有科部、所有同仁的共同責任。 We turned the survey findings into action. A hospital-wide Friendly Medicine campaign moved "friendliness" from being the ID clinic's job to a shared responsibility across every department.

推動形式Format教育・倡議Education · Advocacy

  • 院內倡議文宣與動線標示更新Updated in-house advocacy materials and signage
  • 建立匿名篩檢與隱私動線設計Anonymous screening pathways with privacy by design

2018–19 · 我們與友善的距離 · 問卷調查 2018–19 · The Distance to Friendliness — Hospital Survey

一切的起點。我們透過全院問卷調查，第一次認真量出「友善」與「實際病人經驗」之間的距離，把直覺與抱怨變成可被討論、可被改變的證據。 Where everything began. A hospital-wide questionnaire let us, for the first time, measure the gap between our self-image of "friendliness" and patients' lived experience — turning anecdotes into evidence we could act on.
