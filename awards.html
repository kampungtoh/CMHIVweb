獲獎與肯定 Awards & Recognition 09

外界的看見，是改變的副產品。 External recognition is a by-product of change.

我們不是為了得獎而做，但這些肯定告訴我們：方向是對的。 We do not do this for awards. These recognitions simply tell us: we are heading in the right direction.

疾
2025 · 衛福部疾管署Taiwan CDC

非公務類個人防疫績優獎Outstanding Disease-Prevention Individual (Non-public-sector)

杜漢祥醫師榮獲衛福部疾管署 2025 年度非公務類個人防疫績優獎，肯定其長期投入 HIV 友善醫療與公共衛生的貢獻。Dr. Tu Han-Siong received Taiwan CDC's 2025 Outstanding Disease-Prevention Individual Award (non-public-sector), recognising his long-term contribution to HIV-friendly care and public health.

W
2025 · WHO

參與 WHO 準則制定Contributor to WHO Guidelines

奇美友愛團隊參與 WHO《透過降低 HIV 相關污名與歧視　確保優質醫療品質》準則的制定。Contributed to the WHO guideline Ensuring quality health care by reducing HIV-related stigma and discrimination.

台
2025 · 台灣愛滋病學會Taiwan AIDS Society

年會「友善醫療」實務分享Annual Meeting · Friendly Medicine Practice Session

於 2025 台灣愛滋病學會年會分享「友善醫療」實務經驗。Shared "Friendly Medicine" practice at the 2025 Taiwan AIDS Society Annual Meeting.

金
2023 · NHQA · 國家新創獎・金獎（台灣第一）National Healthcare Quality Award · Gold (1st in Taiwan)

邁向零歧視　跨領域 HIV 全人照護友善醫療Towards Zero Discrimination — Interdisciplinary, Whole-Person, Friendly HIV Care

獎盃照片（請上傳） Trophy photo (please upload) assets/nhqa-trophy.jpg

以「邁向零歧視　跨領域 HIV 全人照護友善醫療」為題，榮獲 2023 國家新創獎金獎，亦是台灣首例。Won the 2023 NHQA Gold Award — the first in Taiwan — for the project "Towards Zero Discrimination: Interdisciplinary, Whole-Person, Friendly HIV Care".

Q
2023 · QIS+D

導入 QIS+D 品質管理模式Adopted the QIS+D Quality-Management Model

導入 QIS+D 品質管理模式，把日常的友善設計轉譯成可被審查的品質指標並獲得肯定，並受邀至日本、泰國及韓國分享。Adopted the QIS+D quality-management model to translate everyday friendly-care design into reviewable quality indicators, earning recognition and invitations to share the work in Japan, Thailand and Korea.
